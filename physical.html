Physical Intelligence

Partner Intelligence for every
physical domain that needs it.

Land, sea, air, and the machines between them. The same mind that reasons through a threaded needle holds a vehicle steady in a dark ocean current — without being retrained for either.

Most physical AI is built one domain at a time — millions of demonstrations, months of training, a model that only knows the task it was fed. Jean OS is built differently.

01

Reasoning, not training

Jean's control decisions come from architectural reasoning — not a policy learned from millions of examples. New domain, same mind. The engine that handles an arm handles an underwater vehicle without starting over.

02

Skip the training bottleneck

When you don't need a massive domain-specific dataset to begin, you don't wait months to start. You move toward deployment faster — measure the plant, reason about it, control it.

03

Every domain costs less than the last

Because the reasoning transfers, each new physical domain reuses the mind already built. Land, sea, air — and eventually humanoids — extend one architecture rather than rebuilding from zero.
